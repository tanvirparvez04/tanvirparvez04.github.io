## 🔬 Research Highlights

My research focuses on developing and evaluating machine learning systems, particularly large language models and data-driven approaches for real-world applications.

- **Remote Research Assistant – MapEval Benchmark (Qatar Computing Research Institute):** Contributed to the development of a benchmark for evaluating geo-spatial reasoning in foundation models. Assisted in data collection, annotation, and evaluation of 28 LLMs on map-based reasoning tasks, leading to a publication accepted at ICML 2025 (Spotlight).

- **LSTM-Based Rice Yield Prediction:** Developed LSTM models to predict rice yields for three rice types using climate variables, achieving R² scores of 0.94, 0.81, and 0.94, outperforming baseline models.

- **MapCoder – Multi-Agent Code Generation:** Assisted in running experiments with prompting techniques to enhance code generation from natural language using LLMs, contributing to dataset generation acknowledged in an ACL conference paper.

- **Machine Learning Regression for Crop Yield Prediction:** Built an end-to-end ML pipeline using historical climate and agricultural data, identifying Random Forest as the best-performing model through comparative analysis.

- **Time Series Forecasting – Bitcoin Price Prediction:** Optimized and validated ARIMA configurations for short-term forecasting, with ARIMA(2,1,2) achieving the best performance (RMSE: 0.0389, MAE: 0.0247).
